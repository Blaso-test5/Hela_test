BC MIP-TAIL STAGING TEST - 2026-11-09

Implementation
- BC staging footprints smaller than one 4x4 compression block now use a
  block-aligned staging mip chain.
- The helper pads the staging texture dimensions by the minimum number of
  mip levels, then maps and copies the mip whose dimensions exactly match
  the D3D12 footprint.
- Both buffer-to-texture upload and texture-to-buffer readback use the
  matching staging subresource. Other formats and BC footprints at least
  4x4 keep the existing staging path.

Verification
- Added d3d11_bc_mip_staging_smoke to CTest.
- Full CTest suite: 5/5 passed.
- Hardware D3D11 feature level: 0xb000.
- BC1 and BC7: upload/readback round-trips passed for 2x2 (mip 1) and
  1x1 (mip 2) subresources in a valid 4x4 mip chain.
- Standalone 2x2 BC staging descriptor remains rejected with E_INVALIDARG
  (0x80070057), as expected; the implementation avoids that descriptor.

Limitations
- This is a D3D11 smoke test, not an Hela runtime verification.
- Hela was launched through Steam with the candidate shader overrides and
  PS-hash tracing. The process initialized and destroyed D3D12 devices,
  then exited before creating PSOs or issuing draws. No shader override
  loading or visible rendering was verified in that attempt.
- The GTX 950M remains untested.
