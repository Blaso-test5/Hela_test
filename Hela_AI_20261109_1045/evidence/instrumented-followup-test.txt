TEXTURE STAGING
- 1,264 staging creation failures were traced to D3D11
  CreateTexture2D; the proxy's 2D depth validation was not the failure.
- All logged failures used CPUAccessFlags=WRITE and a BC format:
  BC1_TYPELESS (70), BC3_TYPELESS (76), BC4_UNORM (80), BC5_UNORM (83),
  BC6H_UF16 (95), or BC7_TYPELESS (97).
- A separate native D3D11 hardware-device probe on this PC reproduced
  E_INVALIDARG for 2x2 staging textures in the tested BC typed/typeless
  formats with both CPU READ and CPU WRITE. The same formats succeeded
  at 4x4 with both access modes. R8G8B8A8_UNORM succeeded at 2x2 and 4x4.
- This isolates the failure to sub-block BC dimensions in the staging
  descriptor, not to the CPU access mode alone. The correct way to stage
  and copy 1x1/2x2 BC mip tails still needs a block-aligned copy test;
  blindly padding the staging texture is not yet validated.

INTERPRETATION
The previously ready PSO #1 is not sufficient to render the observed
scene draws. Most skipped draws use a different missing VS. Separately,
small BC mip uploads fail before the copy/map path. Fix and test these
two blockers independently; do not treat successful Present as proof of
visible output.
